## 🔬 Topics & Research Themes

- **Core Machine Learning**: Supervised, unsupervised, and semi-supervised learning
- **Deep Learning**: Architectures, training methods, generalization theory
- **Reinforcement Learning**: Model-based, model-free, multi-agent RL, RLHF
- **Generative AI**: Diffusion models, GANs, VAEs, flow-based models, large language models
- **Optimization**: Convex/non-convex optimization, stochastic methods, federated optimization
- **Probabilistic Methods**: Bayesian inference, graphical models, causal inference
- **Neuroscience & Cognitive Science**: Computational neuroscience, brain-inspired computing
- **Trustworthy AI**: Fairness, robustness, interpretability, safety, alignment
- **Large Language Models (LLMs)**: Pre-training, fine-tuning, prompting, evaluation
- **Datasets & Benchmarks**: Novel datasets, evaluation protocols, reproducibility
- **AI for Science**: Drug discovery, climate modeling, materials science
- **Computer Vision**: Recognition, generation, 3D understanding
- **Natural Language Processing**: Understanding, generation, multilingual NLP
- **Robotics & Control**: Learning for robotics, sim-to-real transfer
- **Graph Neural Networks**: Node/graph classification, link prediction
- **Privacy & Security**: Differential privacy, adversarial robustness

## 🏆 Awards & Recognition

- **Best Paper Award**: Typically 1–2 papers recognized annually
- **Outstanding Paper Awards**: ~6–10 papers per year
- **Test of Time Award**: Recognizes influential papers from 10 years prior
- NeurIPS is widely considered the most prestigious venue in machine learning, with the highest h5-index of any AI conference

## 💡 Tips for Authors

1. **Start early**: The 9-page limit is tight — plan your paper structure carefully
2. **Novelty is paramount**: NeurIPS values both theoretical novelty and practical impact
3. **Reproducibility checklist**: Complete the mandatory reproducibility checklist thoroughly — reviewers check it
4. **Supplementary matters**: Include code and extended proofs in supplementary material
5. **Broader impact statement**: Required — discuss societal implications thoughtfully
6. **Consider the Datasets track**: If your main contribution is a dataset or benchmark, submit to this dedicated track
7. **Rebuttal period**: Use it wisely — address reviewer concerns with experiments if possible
8. **Dual submission policy**: Papers under review at other venues are generally not allowed
