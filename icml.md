## 🔬 Topics & Research Themes

- **General Machine Learning**: Classification, regression, clustering, dimensionality reduction
- **Representation Learning**: Feature learning, embeddings, self-supervised methods
- **Deep Learning**: Novel architectures, transformers, attention mechanisms, training dynamics
- **Statistical Machine Learning**: Kernel methods, Bayesian methods, non-parametric models
- **Learning Theory**: Generalization bounds, sample complexity, computational learning theory
- **Optimization**: Gradient methods, second-order methods, distributed optimization
- **Reinforcement Learning**: Policy gradient, Q-learning, model-based RL, exploration
- **Natural Language Processing**: Language models, understanding, generation
- **Computer Vision**: Object detection, segmentation, image generation
- **Robotics**: Learning-based control, manipulation, navigation
- **Probabilistic Models**: Variational inference, Monte Carlo methods, normalizing flows
- **Fairness, Accountability & Transparency**: Bias mitigation, explainability
- **Online Learning**: Bandits, online convex optimization
- **Meta-Learning**: Few-shot learning, learning to learn
- **AutoML**: Neural architecture search, hyperparameter optimization
- **Federated & Distributed Learning**: Communication-efficient training, privacy-preserving ML
- **AI for Science**: Scientific discovery applications

## 💡 Tips for Authors

1. **Solid experiments**: ICML values rigorous experimental methodology with proper baselines
2. **Theory appreciated**: Papers with theoretical contributions (convergence proofs, bounds) are highly valued
3. **8-page limit**: Be concise — use the appendix for supplementary proofs and ablations
4. **Code submission**: Strongly encouraged — reviewers appreciate reproducible research
5. **Position for the right audience**: ICML covers broad ML; make sure your paper fits the core ML audience
6. **Rebuttal period**: Respond point-by-point to reviewer concerns with evidence
7. **Check PMLR formatting**: Ensure compliance with the PMLR style file — formatting issues can cause desk rejection
8. **Dual submission**: Not allowed with other conferences; journal submissions may have exceptions
