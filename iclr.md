## 🔬 Topics & Research Themes

- **Representation Learning**: Learning useful representations from data
- **Deep Learning Architectures**: Transformers, CNNs, RNNs, state-space models, mixture of experts
- **Self-Supervised Learning**: Contrastive learning, masked prediction, pre-training strategies
- **Generative Models**: Diffusion models, variational autoencoders, normalizing flows, autoregressive models
- **Reinforcement Learning**: Deep RL, offline RL, RLHF, multi-agent RL
- **AI Safety & Alignment**: Reward modeling, constitutional AI, red-teaming
- **Large Language Models**: Scaling laws, in-context learning, chain-of-thought reasoning
- **Multi-Modal Learning**: Vision-language models, audio-visual learning
- **Graph Neural Networks**: Message passing, equivariant networks, graph transformers
- **Optimization for Deep Learning**: Adam variants, learning rate schedules, loss landscapes
- **Transfer Learning & Domain Adaptation**: Fine-tuning, prompt tuning, adapter methods
- **Theoretical Deep Learning**: Neural tangent kernels, implicit bias, feature learning theory
- **Robustness & Adversarial ML**: Adversarial examples, certified defenses
- **Efficient ML**: Model compression, quantization, knowledge distillation, pruning
- **AI for Science**: Protein folding, molecular design, physics-informed models

## 💡 Tips for Authors

1. **Open review culture**: Your reviews will be public — write papers that engage constructively with the community
2. **Deep learning focus**: ICLR favors papers on representation learning and deep learning; ensure your work fits
3. **Strong empirical results**: Large-scale experiments on established benchmarks strengthen your submission
4. **Theory + practice**: Papers bridging theory and practice (e.g., explaining why methods work) are highly valued
5. **Pre-registration advantage**: Submit your abstract by the abstract deadline — late abstracts are not accepted
6. **Consider the Tiny Papers track**: For preliminary results or novel ideas that don't yet warrant a full paper
7. **Engage during review**: The OpenReview platform allows discussion between authors, reviewers, and the public
8. **Reproducibility**: Submit code and clearly document hyperparameters and experimental setup
