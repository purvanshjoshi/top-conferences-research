## 🔬 Topics & Research Themes

- **Operating Systems**: Kernel design, scheduling, resource management
- **File & Storage Systems**: File systems, key-value stores, persistent memory
- **Distributed Systems**: Consensus, replication, fault tolerance, consistency models
- **Cloud Computing**: Serverless, microservices, container orchestration, resource allocation
- **Mobile & Embedded Systems**: Mobile OS, IoT, edge computing
- **Virtualization**: Hypervisors, containers, unikernels, hardware virtualization
- **Systems for Machine Learning**: Training infrastructure, serving systems, ML compilers
- **Security**: OS security, isolation, sandboxing, trusted execution environments
- **Networking**: Kernel networking, RDMA, programmable switches
- **Energy Efficiency**: Power management, green computing
- **Reliability**: Bug detection, crash consistency, formal verification of systems

## 🏆 Awards & Recognition

- **Best Paper Award**: Typically 1–2 papers per edition

## 💡 Tips for Authors

1. **Systems building is essential**: SOSP strongly favors papers that build and evaluate real systems
2. **Artifact evaluation**: Submit artifacts — papers with reproducible artifacts are viewed more favorably
3. **End-to-end evaluation**: Show performance on real workloads, not just microbenchmarks
4. **Novelty + impact**: The bar is extremely high; your system should solve a real, important problem in a new way
5. **Strong writing**: With 16–18% acceptance, clear and compelling writing makes a significant difference
6. **Comparison with state-of-art**: Thorough comparison with existing systems is expected
7. **Open-source**: Releasing code alongside your paper strengthens the contribution
8. **Single-track format**: Your talk will be seen by everyone — prepare it well
